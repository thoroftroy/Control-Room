# Control Room changelog

## v0.5.0

- Fixed the Settings tab crashing when you scrolled down to Apps
- Crash reports can be sent to the Wonderdesk developer: turn on automatic sending, or send the waiting ones with one tap. “What gets sent” shows each report exactly as it would go out
- Works properly in landscape: tabs move to a rail at the side so the content keeps its height, and screens that ran out of room now scroll
- If the app ever crashes it says so next time it opens and offers to send a report (only the error, app version and phone model, with file names and links removed). Turn on automatic sending in Control Room › Wonderdesk
- Lists can no longer crash when two items share a name

## v0.4.0

- New Crash reports panel in the Wonderdesk tab: crashes and freezes from any Wonderdesk app are collected with their logs, ready to share, copy or clear
- If the app ever crashes or freezes, a report goes to Control Room so it can be shared and fixed
- App icon art is larger and fills its tile without being cut off

## v0.3.1

- New: Apps Wonderdesk replaces. Finds the stock and third-party apps each installed Wonderdesk app takes over (Files, Photos, Calendar, Clock, Keep, Calculator, VLC and more) and archives, uninstalls or disables the ones you choose, one prompt at a time
- Default apps shortcut to pick Wonderdesk apps for opening files, photos and links
- App icon redrawn so nothing is cut off: round icons fill the space, the rest sit on an Aqua tile
- Smoother and lighter on the battery: the window background no longer animates, tap effects sleep until you tap, and nothing ticks while the app is in the background
- Opening the app no longer waits on Control Room to read the suite look

## v0.3.0

- One look for every Wonderdesk app
- Quick controls for torch, Do Not Disturb, rotation, volume and brightness
- Searchable list of Android settings and a Wonderdesk tab with an Apphub link
