# Control Room for Android

Control Room is a shortcut panel for Android settings plus this app's appearance. By Rednave.

## Install

Requires Android 10 or newer (best on Android 13+).

1. Copy the APK file to your phone (USB, Bluetooth, cloud drive — whatever you like) and tap it.
2. Allow "install from unknown sources" when asked.
3. Open the app from your app drawer.

## Using Control Room

- Search the list to find a shortcut, then tap it to open the matching official Android settings screen (display, sound, Wi-Fi, battery saver, date and time, apps, about phone and more).
- Change this app's appearance and motion under Appearance.

Control Room opens system settings; it does not replace them, the lock screen or the notification shade.

## Look and feel

Open About inside the app to choose between Aqua, Bliss, Dark Bliss and Super, and to adjust motion. Aqua is the default.
